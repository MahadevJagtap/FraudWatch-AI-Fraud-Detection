What Financial Fraud Detection Means

Financial fraud detection is the automated process of analyzing electronic transaction streams in real time to intercept unauthorized balance transfers, account takeovers, and laundering attempts before funds irrevocably leave the payments network.

How FraudWatch Analyzes Transactions

Every transaction is evaluated using gradient-boosted decision trees trained on PaySim financial behaviors. The engine engineers origin drain ratios, balance integrity flags, simulation hour cycles, recipient merchant classification, and Feast online account history.

Risk Categories Under Configured Demo Policy

Low Risk (< 0.50): Automated approval for routine spending.
Medium Risk (0.50 – 0.89): Flagged for human review; funds placed on temporary hold.
High Risk (≥ 0.90): Automated block; immediate critical alert persisted to SQLite cases store.

Why Analysts Review Suspicious Cases

Machine learning flags anomalies with high sensitivity, but human analysts apply contextual judgment. FraudWatch logs every alert to an immutable audit trail where investigators record dispositions, confirm real attacks, and prevent false customer friction.
